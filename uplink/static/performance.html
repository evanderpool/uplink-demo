Keyword vs. hybrid retrieval

The headline result: adding semantic vectors to keyword search, measured on the same 15 golden questions over the ten Apple 10-K filings.

15-question set, on-device / API embeddings, deterministic across runs. Near-duplicate filings are the case where keyword search is most disadvantaged (it matches common words and loses the year), so this gain would likely be smaller on a corpus of distinct documents — reported honestly rather than as a universal number.

Where hybrid changed the answer

Per-question ranks, BM25 → hybrid, on every question whose result moved. A rank of 1 is the correct filing first; 0 is a miss. Nothing regressed.

The SG&A-2022 row is the clearest case: keyword search returned the 2016, 2017, and 2025 filings — it matched the words "selling, general, administrative" and lost the year. Hybrid understood the question was about 2022 and returned that filing first.

How the honesty is enforced

100%published answers with a verified citation
by indexcitations picked by chunk number, coordinates copied from the index
refusedany answer citing an unindexed or out-of-scope document
"not measured"what the panel shows for anything unmeasured — never a guess

Grounding is a property of the pipeline, not a prompt: every citation is checked against the index before an answer is shown, so a claim attributed to a document that isn't there — or one the visitor deselected — cannot be published at all.
